Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about using Rent Ease.

How do I find a property?

Open the Properties page, choose a property type or budget, and browse the sample listings.

How can I ask about a property?

Select “Ask about it” on a listing to open our Contact page and find the contact details.

Can I list my property?

Yes. Visit the Landlord page to see the basic steps for sharing a rental listing.

Are the listed properties real and available?

The listings in this sample website are examples for a student project. Confirm all details and availability before making any rental decision.

Can I view different kinds of rentals?

Yes. Use the Categories dropdown to explore apartments, houses and single rooms.
